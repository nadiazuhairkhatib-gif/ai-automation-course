# Your First Attempt

## Before You Learn the Tools

You have read the challenge.

Now try to solve it.

**Do not use ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Zapier, or any automation tool yet.**

There is no single correct answer at this stage.

We want to understand how you think about the problem before introducing the technology.

---

## 1. What Is Happening?

In your own words:

**What is the actual problem in this scenario?**

Write 2–3 sentences.

---

## 2. What Happens Today?

Describe the current process step by step.

Start with:

> Someone receives...

Then continue until:

> The work is completed.

---

## 3. Where Does the Work Slow Down?

Identify at least **3 points** where the process could become:

* slow
* repetitive
* confusing
* error-prone
* difficult to track

Explain why.

---

## 4. Where Are Decisions Made?

Identify at least **2 decisions** someone needs to make.

For each decision:

**What information is needed?**

**What determines the decision?**

**Who currently makes it?**

---

## 5. What Could Be Handled by Technology?

For each important step, make your first hypothesis:

| Process Step | AI | Rule | Human | Why?           |
| ------------ | -- | ---- | ----- | -------------- |
| Example      | ?  | ?    | ?     | Your reasoning |
| Step 1       |    |      |       |                |
| Step 2       |    |      |       |                |
| Step 3       |    |      |       |                |

You are allowed to change your answer later.

---

## 6. Sketch Your Process

Draw or write your first version of the process:

```text
Input
  ↓
Step
  ↓
Decision
  ↓
Action
  ↓
Output
```

Do not worry about making it technically correct.

We want to see your current mental model.

---

## 7. What Could Go Wrong?

Choose one step.

Complete:

**I think this step could fail because...**

**If it fails, the impact could be...**

**Someone should detect the problem by...**

---

## 8. Your First Question

After attempting the challenge, write **one question** you now have.

Examples:

* Could AI understand this type of information?
* How would we connect the different steps?
* When should AI make a decision?
* How can a human review the result?
* What happens when the information is incomplete?

Write your own question.

---

## Important

Your first attempt is **not a test of whether you already know AI automation**.

It is evidence of how you currently think about a work process.

You will return to this attempt later.

After learning the concepts and building the solution, you will compare:

> **What I thought before → What I understand now**
