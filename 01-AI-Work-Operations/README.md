# U1 — AI Work Operations System

**Capability:** Process Thinking
**Core Question:** How does the work actually happen?

## Why This Unit Matters

AI automation does not begin with a tool.

It begins with understanding how work actually happens.

In this unit, you will learn to look at everyday work as a process:

**Information → Process → Decision → Action → Output**

You will learn to identify where AI can help, where rules are better, and where a human should remain in control.

---

## What You Will Learn

By the end of this unit, you will be able to:

* distinguish a **Task** from a **Process**
* identify **Inputs, Transformations, Decisions, and Outputs**
* map how information moves through a process
* identify repetitive work and decision points
* decide when to use **AI, Rules, or Human judgment**
* design a process before choosing a tool
* create structured AI outputs
* include **Human-in-the-Loop** review
* test an AI-assisted workflow
* identify failures and improve the workflow
* define the first version of your own **NOVA** solution

---

## What You Will Build

You will build a small AI-assisted work process that transforms unstructured information into a useful structured output.

### Guided Build

**Meeting Notes → Action Plan**

```text
Meeting Notes
      ↓
AI Extraction
      ↓
Structured Actions
      ↓
Human Review
      ↓
Google Sheet / Task List
```

You will then build an independent variation using a different work scenario.

---

## Your Learning Journey

You will not begin by learning the tools.

You will begin with the problem.

```text
Challenge
    ↓
Attempt
    ↓
Concept
    ↓
Guided Build
    ↓
Test
    ↓
Break
    ↓
Diagnose & Fix
    ↓
Independent Build
    ↓
NOVA Transfer
    ↓
Prove
```

The goal is not simply to make the workflow work.

The goal is to understand **why it works, where it can fail, and how to improve it.**

## NOVA

### NOVA Stage: Problem & Process Definition

In this unit, you will not build your complete NOVA solution.

You will define its foundation:

* User
* Problem
* Current Process
* Pain Points
* Desired Outcome
* Initial AI / Rule / Human opportunities

Your first goal is to understand the problem before deciding what technology to use.
